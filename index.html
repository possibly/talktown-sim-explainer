The 140-Year Timeline
How the simulation allocates fidelity across world history

Talktown simulates 140 years of small-town American life before the player ever touches it. Rather than simulate every single day at full cost, it uses a three-phase strategy: a stochastic lo-fi pass for almost all of history, a knowledge-implantation bridge, then a full-fidelity final week.

The lo-fi pass still handles births, deaths, marriages, divorces, retirements, and business openings on every timestep — only the expensive routine + socialise cycle is gated behind the 0.5% roll. When a lo-fi timestep does fire, it passes missing_timesteps_to_account_for = days_since_last_sim × 2 into socialize() to scale up relationship progression proportionally.

03
Routine Decision Tree
The full logic of Routine.decide_where_to_go()

Every character has a Routine object. On each timestep, enact() calls decide_where_to_go(), which cascades through a series of conditionals to pick both a destination and an occasion label. Hover any node to see the underlying code.

occasion values recorded in Whereabouts:
'work' · 'school' · 'home' · 'errand' · 'leisure'

The "go public" branch itself forks probabilistically between errands and visits. For errands, the destination type is drawn from probabilities_of_errand_for_service_type, and the specific business is chosen by proximity (closest to home or work) vs. random selection.

For visits, the relationship type (neighbor / friend / immediate family / extended family) is drawn from who_someone_visiting_will_visit_probabilities. Friends are ranked by relationship.charge — best friend wins 50% of the time.

Social Interaction Probability
How personality and relationships shape _decide_to_instigate_social_interaction()

When two characters share a location, each independently rolls to decide whether to instigate an interaction. The roll probability is assembled from two components — a personality factor and a relationship factor — then clamped to [floor, cap].
